About this atlas

Mission: One simple map that answers “who ruled here in this year?” for the whole Northeast — for students, teachers and curious readers.

Method: We place the best-known ruling house for each area in each year, using published atlases and histories. Where control was shared, seasonal or tribal, we show grey or note it as tributary.

Limits: Borders are approximations. Hill polities — Naga, Mizo, Khasi and others — were often confederacies of villages and chiefs, not single kingdoms with fixed borders. Dates with “c.” are approximate.

How to read this map

  • Colour = ruler. Each colour is one kingdom / power.
  • Dashed border = tributary. It kept its own chief but paid tribute to a bigger power. Turn on “Overlords” to flood it with the overlord’s colour.
  • Grey = no single house. Often tribal village polities or disputed land.
  • Slider = time. Drag it and the map redraws for that year.
  • Click = story. Click any colour to read its capital, kings and battles.
